# Stoneflow Coatings: See It In Your Kitchen (demo)

A worktop visualiser demo made for Stoneflow Coatings by Amy Conlon. Customers take a photo of their kitchen, outline the worktop, try different resin finishes on it, and send the picture for a quote.

- Live demo: https://amyconlon.github.io/stoneflow-visualiser/
- This is a demo, not a live page. The finishes are generic textures until Stoneflow supplies photos of its real range.
- Photos stay on the customer's phone unless they choose to share them.
- All settings (business name, colours, contact links and finishes) are in the settings block near the bottom of `index.html`.
